## The Protocol Was the Bottleneck

The existing DICOM retrieval system relied on C-FIND — a protocol designed in the 1990s for sequential, blocking queries. Every patient lookup required establishing an association, sending a query, waiting for the response, and tearing down the connection. No batching. No parallelism. No caching layer.
